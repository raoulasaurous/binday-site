# Wheelie Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 August 2026_

Wheelie is built to know as little about you as possible.

## What the app does with your data

- **Your postcode and address choice** are used only to look up bin-collection
  schedules. The postcode is sent to postcodes.io (an open-data service run on
  Office for National Statistics data) to identify your council, and to your
  council's own bin-collection service to list addresses and collection dates.
  These requests go **directly from your device** to those services; they do
  not pass through any server operated by us.
- **Your saved address, schedule, and reminder settings** are stored only on
  your device (and in your device's app-group container so the widget can read
  them). They never leave your device except as described above.
- **Reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push
  service is involved.
- **An anonymous monthly count.** Once a month the app tells us your council
  area (its public code, e.g. "E07000008") and the app version. That is the
  whole message — no name, no address, no postcode, no device identifier, and
  nothing that could link one month's count to the next. We use it to see
  which councils Wheelie is actually serving so we know where to focus. **You
  can switch this off in Settings**, and the app sends nothing.
- **Service notices.** The app downloads a small public file from wheelie.day
  (for example, a notice about a bin strike in a council area). This is a
  plain download; no personal data is sent with it.

## What we don't do

- No accounts. No sign-up. No passwords.
- No tracking, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics SDKs.
- Nothing we receive can identify you, and nothing is ever sold or shared.
- Tips are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details,
  and the app keeps no record of whether you tipped.

## Third-party services

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| postcodes.io | The postcode you type | To identify your council |
| Your council's bin-lookup service | Your postcode, then your chosen address's property reference | To list addresses and fetch collection dates |
| Our usage counter (Cloudflare) | Your council's public area code + app version, once a month, if enabled | To count which councils Wheelie serves |

Each service's own privacy policy governs its handling of those requests.

## Data removal

Delete the app and everything it stored is gone. The monthly count contains
nothing that identifies you, so there is nothing about you to request or
delete — we hold no personal data.

## Support emails

If you email support (including via the app's "report a problem" form), we
receive your email address and whatever you chose to include in the message.
It's used only to help with your report, kept only in our mailbox, and never
shared or added to any list.
